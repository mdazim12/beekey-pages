2D animation FAQs.

A few common questions about timelines, formats and how the process works. You can always ask us more.

How long does it take to create a 2D animation?

For shorter videos (up to 60 seconds), most projects take around 1–2 weeks from script approval, depending on complexity and feedback speed. Longer or more complex projects can take a bit more time.

Do you provide the voiceover and music?

Yes. We can handle voiceover casting and recording in a range of languages and styles, plus background music and sound effects that fit your brand and audience.

Can you match our existing brand style or characters?

Absolutely. We can work from your brand guidelines, existing character designs or marketing materials to ensure everything feels consistent.

What formats will we receive the final video in?

Typically we deliver MP4 files optimized for web and social, plus any additional formats you need for presentations or ad platforms.

Can we reuse the assets from the animation elsewhere?

Yes. On request, we can also provide selected illustrations or characters as separate files so you can reuse them in slides, banners or future campaigns.
