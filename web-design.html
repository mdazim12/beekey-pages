Web design FAQs

A few common questions we’re happy to answer up front. Ask us anything else during your free consultation.

How long does it take to build a website?

For a typical small business site, most projects take around 10–14 days from kickoff to launch, depending on feedback speed and content readiness.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can either refresh your current structure or start from scratch if it makes more sense. We’ll give you an honest recommendation after the audit.

Do you also provide hosting and domain?

We can recommend hosting providers and help you connect everything. You remain the owner of your domain and hosting account.

Can you help with website copy and images?

Absolutely. Many clients ask us to suggest headlines, structure and stock imagery. We can also work with your existing assets.

What platform will my site be built on?

We choose the best fit based on your needs (for example, a user-friendly CMS). If you already have a preferred platform, we can work with that too.
